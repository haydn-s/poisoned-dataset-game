## Debrief prompts

1. Explain why the learner selected its final rule. Which training labels mattered?
2. Which decision felt consequential? Which felt obvious or arbitrary?
3. Did an audit find poison without changing the selected rule?
4. How could an attacker hide poisoning from a conflict-based strategy?
5. Rate clarity and enjoyment separately from 1 to 5, and explain each rating in a sentence.
6. What single change would make the next match easier to understand or more interesting?

## Revision decision

Record the observed problem, proposed change, and a concrete check for the next walkthrough. Preserve observations that contradict the preferred design. Report actual session counts, intervention counts, and unresolved problems; do not infer measured learning gains from debrief answers or enjoyment ratings.
