Before production

  • Print the dielines on 11 x 17 in cardstock at 100%, score the folds, and fit the real kit before choosing final dimensions.
  • Board thickness changes the clearance: rigid box board needs about 0.1 in more than cardstock.
  • Confirm the supplier's own template, bleed, safe area, proof process, price and delivery date against the November 23 deadline before ordering.
  • Unused truth and reference cards go back face down in the reference well between rounds.
